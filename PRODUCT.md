# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Affluent homeowners planning a significant whole-home residential project. They are time-poor and quality-driven, comparing studios on taste, trust, and confidence that the work will actually be delivered. International/European market, evaluated in neutral international English.

## Product Purpose

A marketing landing page for a turnkey residential interior design studio. It exists to turn qualified homeowners into project enquiries by demonstrating taste and end-to-end delivery. Success means a homeowner recognises their own ambition in the work and starts a conversation.

## Positioning

Turnkey full-service: one studio owns design, procurement, and installation, so the client receives a finished home instead of drawings plus a list of contractors.

## Operating Context

European/international market. Whole-home projects are the flagship offer. Visitors evaluate the portfolio first, then process and trust; contact is secondary but always available. The first step after the page is a discovery call or project enquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single public marketing landing page; no authenticated app or transactional flows. Existing stack: Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn, React Three Fiber/Three.js, and GSAP are available. No studio-owned project photography, logo, client testimonials, commissioned case studies, press, pricing, or team facts exist. Licensed reference photography may be used when it is labelled as reference material, credited in the page and in `src/lib/photography.ts`, and never presented as a client commission or completed project. Lorem-ipsum-style illustrative assets may also be created. Client names, quotes, benchmarks, awards, and pricing claims must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Provisional studio name: Atelier Aveline (invented at the user's request; replaceable). Voice: composed, precise, and warm, with no hard-sell hype. No existing logo, wordmark, or brand guidelines.

## Evidence on Hand

Licensed reference photography in `public/images/`, credited in the page and in `src/lib/photography.ts`. No studio-owned commissions or client projects. Future work may produce additional illustrative or licensed reference imagery, but it must not be presented as real completed projects, and it must not fabricate testimonials, client identities, or credentials.

## Product Principles

1. Show the finished home, not the mood board: the portfolio is the argument.
2. Make turnkey confidence legible: design, procurement, and installation under one accountable team.
3. Persuade the time-poor: short, decisive copy and a low-friction next step.
4. Taste over volume: fewer, better projects presented with restraint.
5. Honest assets: reference and illustrative work is labelled and credited; proof is never invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: sufficient contrast, full keyboard access, visible focus, and reduced-motion support for any 3D or animated content.
